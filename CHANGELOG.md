## 0.3.0 — naive-icons 1.17.0 (2026-11-25)

- **Breaking, icons:** `IslandGlyph.boxingGlove` is removed; upstream replaced the glyph with `boxing` (`IslandGlyph.boxing`).
- **Icons:** naive-icons 1.17.0: 936 glyphs, 228 added, and archery, skateboard, ski and snowboard show the new artwork. The standing paint rules settle the new roles; 42 roles in 23 added glyphs are held for the owner and keep upstream paint (`docs/icon-paint-roles.json` `import_v1_17.pending`).
- **Scrollbar:** Home and End reach the ends on every platform (the Web had no binding). The focus ring shows for keyboard use only, not after a pointer press.
- **Gallery:** the Scrollbar page was compared side by side with a local build of the upstream demo: its rows are 16px on a 25px line, as the reference. Added icons show their English name, as the reference does.

## 0.2.0 — Scrollbar (2026-11-24)

The reference pin moves to upstream main six commits after 4.0.1 (unreleased; `docs/reference-gallery.json`).

- **New, Scrollbar:** `IslandScrollbar` and `IslandScrollbarDirection`. A scroll area with an 8px pill thumb that is always shown while the content overflows, darkens under the pointer and takes its thickness out of the content; vertical, horizontal or both axes; optional track, thumb colour and thickness; `padding` inside the scrolling content; borrowed `verticalController` and `horizontalController`. The area is a focus stop for keyboard scrolling, and a wheel at its end does not move an outer scrollable. Built from the upstream source; the reference page is not published yet, so no side-by-side comparison exists.
- **Gallery:** new Scrollbar page (64 routes). The Skill page plays its circle Loading only when entered from the home page.
- Not taken over: naive-icons 1.17.0. The icon set stays at 1.14.0 (709 glyphs).

## 0.1.0 — reference 4.0.0 port (2026-11-21)

The reference is re-pinned from 2.2.1 to 4.0.0 (`docs/reference-gallery.json`). Experimental: widget tests cover the ports and new components below, and the Web and Windows builds, the Windows-engine entries and the reference comparisons recorded in `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md` were run with the limits stated there; every status stays `NOT_ACCEPTED`. Open measurements and owner decisions are in `docs/DESIGN.md#pending-owner-decisions-400`.

- **Behaviour change, Select:** new `variant` (`IslandSelectVariant`). `normal` is the new default: the panel opens centred below the trigger, flips above when below is short and above is larger, and aligns to the trigger edge on an overflowing side. `game` is the previous side popover (beside the trigger, vertically centred). Existing callers get `dropdown`; pass `variant: IslandSelectVariant.game` to keep the old placement. Panel styling, width, focus and selection are identical in both.
- **Behaviour change, formats:** `IslandDatePicker.format`, `IslandTimePicker.format` and `IslandTimeValue.format` now expand the dayjs-style core tokens (`YYYY`, `YY`, `M`–`MMMM`, `D`, `DD`, `d`–`dddd`, `H`, `HH`, `h`, `hh`, `a`, `A`, `m`, `mm`, `s`, `ss`, `SSS`, `Z`, `ZZ`; English names) and `[text]` renders literally. Previously only `YYYY`/`MM`/`DD`/`M`/`D` (date) and `HH`/`mm`/`ss` (time) were replaced, so any other token letter in an existing pattern, such as the `a` in `at`, now renders; escape literal text as `[at]`. `IslandTimePicker` shows the seconds column when an unescaped `ss` is present. New `IslandTimeLocale` (`en`, `zhCn`) selects the language of month, weekday and meridiem names.
- **Breaking, localization:** every built-in string now comes from `IslandLocalizations` (Chinese and English). Parameters that defaulted to Chinese are nullable and null means the localized default: Select `label`, `placeholder`, `clearLabel`, `notFound`; DatePicker and TimePicker `placeholder`, `label`; Drawer and Notification `closeLabel`; Rate and BackTop `semanticLabel`; Upload `label`. An app without the delegates or with a non-`zh` locale now shows English where it showed Chinese; register `IslandLocalizations.delegates` with `Locale('zh')` to keep the reference text.
- **Behaviour change, Chart tooltip:** `builder` content wider than the 260px panel now widens it up to min(400, viewport width − 16) instead of being laid out at 228px; the default content is unchanged.
