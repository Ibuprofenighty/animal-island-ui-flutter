# Maintenance / 维护

The package version in `pubspec.yaml` is experimental. Breaking public behavior requires a minor version and migration notes; patch releases retain contracts. Public tokens, semantics, event timing and focus behavior are API. Use one implementation and update contracts, consumers, tests and documentation atomically, without compatibility shims.

CI configuration is maintained with the source. A local result does not prove hosted CI passed. Keep evidence immutable and source-bound; use unique attempt names and record launch/completion status. Re-run affected checks after source changes. Historical outputs and missing manual evidence must not be counted as current passes. The repository owner supplies issue/security reporting and support terms for external distribution.

## Icon import / 图标导入

All 936 `IslandGlyph` values (naive-icons v1.17.0, commit `55978e414455e69c1997a5c424554699aca7ab34`) come from `python -B tool/import_icons.py`, the one maintained importer; do not hand-edit `assets/icons`, `lib/src/foundation/icon_names.dart` or the icon entries of `docs/asset-provenance.json`. It reads a local git checkout of the upstream repository at `scratch/naive-icons` (both the previous and the current pin must be present) and the authored assets from this repository's baseline commit, and rewrites those three outputs plus the record of its import step in `docs/icon-paint-roles.json`. The counts in the rest of this paragraph are those of the 1.14.0 step (709 glyphs, record `import_v1_14`); the next paragraph gives the 1.17.0 step. Per glyph it keeps the authored asset when upstream is unchanged and otherwise settles paint in this order. R1 maps the root ink `#2A2A2A` to `currentColor`. Authored carry re-applies each authored per-attribute repair onto a redrawn glyph where the element's geometry and the attribute are unchanged upstream; the other repairs are dropped and listed (`carried.<name>.repairs_dropped`). Role carry covers a glyph whose element count and tag sequence are unchanged upstream: an element at the same position with unchanged paint attributes is the same role on moved geometry, so its authored repairs are carried (reason `role carry`) and, when its measured role is unchanged too, the earlier per-glyph decision stands (`carried.<name>.roles_settled_by_role_carry`; 50 repairs across 21 glyphs, 147 roles settled). The owner rules of 2026-11-17 then settle the remaining element roles in bulk, applying the previous round's majority dispositions, each recorded with its rule under `import_v1_14.settled`: O1, an explicit coloured stroke reaching the host becomes `currentColor` (263 elements) unless its colour is the meaning (`SEMANTIC_COLOUR`: the red negation slashes of cloud-off, location-off, mic-off, phone-off and video-off); O2, a fixed `#2A2A2A` stroke reaching the host becomes `currentColor` (174); O3, an inherited interior stroke becomes fixed ink `#2A2A2A` only where that keeps 3:1 on every measured own backing (45; lowest measured 4.32:1); O4, the host-reaching contour of a fixed silhouette follows `currentColor` (47 glyphs, all satisfied through O1/O2); O5, interior detail under 3:1 on its own backing keeps the upstream paint as decorative (291). The owner rules of 2026-11-18 settle the roles those rules left: O6, each `SEMANTIC_COLOUR` slash keeps its hue and moves along OKLCH lightness (chroma and hue fixed, clipped to sRGB only if needed) to the first 8-bit colour that reaches 3:1 on light paper `#F8F8F0`, the least change that passes (5 elements, `#E76F51` → `#E46C4F`); the importer stops if that colour misses 3:1 on light paper, dark paper or dark canvas, and records the light-canvas ratio, which stays under 3:1, and the ratios where the slash crosses its own body paint, which O6 does not change; O7, the roles listed in `OWNER_KEPT` keep upstream paint by owner decision (15: eleven inherited interior strokes, four coloured-fill silhouettes), each with the measurement that stopped O1–O5, and the importer stops if a listed role no longer arises in its listed category. `REPAIRS` holds the authored repairs on redrawn geometry, currently one, the eye-off clip split (`currentColor` outside the eye, `#E05A5A` inside the cream body, upstream `#E76F51` kept over the iris), measured per segment and recorded under `import_v1_14.repairs`. A role no rule settles is held: it keeps upstream paint and is written to `import_v1_14.pending` instead of being guessed (none at present), and a held item never changes paint. The importer re-measures its own output and aborts if a rule is not met (every O1/O2/O4 element must reach the host in `currentColor`, every O3 element must keep 3:1 on its own backing, every O6 element must reach the host in its derived colour). A full run takes about two minutes; `--check` lists the outputs that would change without writing, reproduces them and exits 1 if any would differ. Because a re-run regenerates the assets from upstream and the baseline, an owner decision on a role must be made in the importer (the rules, `SEMANTIC_COLOUR`, `OWNER_KEPT`, `REPAIRS`), not in an SVG. The O7 roles are not covered by the contrast matrices: the filled host-contour matrix covers 655 of the 659 filled glyphs (the four O7 silhouettes are listed in the test with their reasons) and the transparent matrix all 50 glyphs without fill. The outcomes are measured and were accepted by the owner as a set on 2026-11-19, not glyph by glyph, including role-carried decisions on redrawn geometry such as the owl and penguin eyes (see [DESIGN](DESIGN.md#owner-decisions-2026-11-18)). Earlier sections of `docs/icon-paint-roles.json` describe the 163 earlier assets, and a glyph with dropped repairs now has a different one.

The 1.17.0 step (2026-11-25, record `import_v1_17`) runs the same importer with the 1.14.0 result as its baseline. 704 glyphs are kept byte for byte and their settlement is still the one in `import_v1_14`; 4 redrawn glyphs (archery, skateboard, ski, snowboard) carry 11 authored repairs and drop 7; 228 glyphs are added; `boxing-glove`, which upstream replaced with `boxing`, is removed (listed under `removed`).
